# The Atlas Body of Knowledge (ABOK)

## Introduction

The **Atlas Body of Knowledge (ABOK)** represents the organized collection of concepts, principles, methodologies, engineering models, and best practices that define the emerging discipline of **Curriculum Engineering**.

The Atlas Handbook serves as the primary publication of the ABOK. Together with supporting standards, specifications, templates, governance documents, and reference implementations, it establishes a comprehensive and evolving body of professional knowledge for designing, developing, governing, and continuously improving educational systems.

Like other mature engineering disciplines, Curriculum Engineering requires more than tools and processes. It requires a shared vocabulary, common principles, established methodologies, and a structured body of knowledge that enables practitioners to communicate, collaborate, and advance the discipline.

The ABOK provides that foundation.

---

# Purpose of the ABOK

The Atlas Body of Knowledge exists to:

- Define the core concepts of Curriculum Engineering.
- Establish a common professional vocabulary.
- Organize knowledge into logical domains.
- Support consistent application of engineering principles to education.
- Provide a foundation for Atlas Standards and Specifications.
- Guide professional practice.
- Support research and continuous improvement.
- Enable future certification and professional development.

The ABOK is intended to evolve alongside the discipline it describes.

---

# Knowledge Areas

The Atlas Body of Knowledge is organized into a series of Knowledge Areas (KAs).

Each Knowledge Area represents a major domain of Curriculum Engineering and contains related concepts, engineering models, methodologies, and recommended practices.

Together, the Knowledge Areas provide a complete view of the discipline.

---

# Knowledge Area Overview

## KA1 — Foundations of Curriculum Engineering

Establishes the rationale, philosophy, principles, and historical context of Curriculum Engineering.

Topics include:

- The Case for Curriculum Engineering
- History of Curriculum Development
- Atlas Philosophy
- Systems Thinking
- Engineering Principles

---

## KA2 — Knowledge Engineering

Examines how expertise is identified, analyzed, structured, and transformed into educational systems.

Topics include:

- Knowledge Domains
- Subject Matter Expertise
- Competency Engineering
- Knowledge Transformation
- Knowledge Modeling

---

## KA3 — Educational Systems Engineering

Introduces the systems perspective of educational design.

Topics include:

- Educational Systems
- Program Architecture

- Course Architecture
- Educational Components
- Interfaces and Dependencies

---

## KA4 — Curriculum Lifecycle Engineering

Presents the Atlas Method for engineering educational systems.

The lifecycle consists of six phases:

- Discover
- Engineer
- Design
- Develop
- Deliver
- Improve

This Knowledge Area forms the operational core of the Atlas Framework.

---

# Relationship to the Atlas Framework

The Atlas Body of Knowledge provides the conceptual foundation upon which the Atlas Framework is built.

The relationship between the two can be summarized as follows:

| Component | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Atlas Body of Knowledge | Defines what practitioners should know. |
| Atlas Method | Defines how Curriculum Engineering is performed. |
| Atlas Standards | Define required practices. |
| Atlas Specifications | Explain implementation details. |
| Atlas Templates | Provide reusable engineering artifacts. |
| Reference Implementations | Demonstrate practical application. |
| Atlas Studio | Automates and supports implementation. |

The ABOK informs every other component of the Atlas ecosystem.

# A Living Body of Knowledge

The Atlas Body of Knowledge is intended to evolve through research, professional practice, technological advancement, and community collaboration.

As Curriculum Engineering matures, additional Knowledge Areas, concepts, engineering models, and recommended practices may be incorporated into future editions.

This commitment to continuous improvement ensures that the ABOK remains relevant, authoritative, and responsive to the changing needs of education and industry.
