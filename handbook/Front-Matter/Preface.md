# Preface

Education has become increasingly complex. Curriculum must now respond
simultaneously to accreditation, workforce expectations, technological
change, learner diversity, analytics, and continuous improvement.

This handbook introduces **Curriculum Engineering**, an emerging
engineering discipline concerned with the systematic design, governance,
implementation, assessment, maintenance, and continuous improvement of
educational systems.

The **Atlas Curriculum Engineering Framework (ACEF)** is presented
throughout this handbook as one implementation of the principles and
practices of Curriculum Engineering. Rather than replacing educational
theory or instructional design, Atlas integrates those contributions
with systems thinking, lifecycle management, governance, quality
assurance, documentation, and traceability.

The purpose of this handbook is therefore twofold:

-   To establish the Body of Knowledge for Curriculum Engineering.
-   To explain how the Atlas Framework implements that body of
    knowledge.

The chapters that follow represent the beginning of a living body of
knowledge that will mature through research, collaboration, and
professional practice.
