# How to Use This Handbook

## Introduction

The Atlas Handbook is the primary publication of the Atlas Body of
Knowledge (ABOK) for Curriculum Engineering.

The handbook introduces the discipline first and the Atlas Framework
second.

## Organization

The handbook is organized into **eight Knowledge Areas (KAs)**. Each
Knowledge Area represents a major domain of Curriculum Engineering and
builds upon the concepts introduced in the preceding Knowledge Areas.

Readers new to the discipline are encouraged to study the Knowledge
Areas sequentially before consulting individual chapters as reference
material.

## Relationship to Atlas

The handbook defines the discipline.

The Atlas Framework implements the discipline.

Atlas Standards establish requirements.

Atlas Specifications describe implementation.

Atlas Studio provides software support for practitioners.
