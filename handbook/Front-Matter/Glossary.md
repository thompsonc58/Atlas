# Glossary

## Purpose

The Atlas Glossary establishes the official terminology used throughout the Atlas Curriculum Engineering Framework (ACEF).

These definitions provide a common vocabulary for educators, curriculum developers, instructional designers, researchers, software developers, and other practitioners.

Unless otherwise specified, the definitions contained within this glossary shall take precedence over informal usage.

The glossary is intended to evolve as the discipline of Curriculum Engineering matures.

---

# A

## Academic Program

A structured collection of courses and learning experiences designed to achieve defined educational outcomes and award a credential.

---

## Assessment

A process used to measure learner achievement against defined learning outcomes or competencies.

---

## Assessment Artifact

Any deliverable produced by a learner for the purpose of demonstrating achievement of one or more learning outcomes.

---

## Atlas Framework

The complete collection of methodologies, standards, specifications, templates, governance documents, software, and reference implementations that support Curriculum Engineering.

---

## Atlas Handbook

The authoritative body of knowledge describing the principles, philosophy, methodology, and engineering models of Curriculum Engineering.

---

## Atlas Method

The lifecycle methodology used to engineer educational systems.

The six phases are:

- Discover
- Engineer
- Design
- Develop
- Deliver
- Improve

---

## Atlas Studio

The software platform that supports implementation of the Atlas Framework.

---

# C

## Competency

A measurable combination of knowledge, skills, and abilities that a learner is expected to demonstrate.

---

## Continuous Improvement

The ongoing process of evaluating, refining, and enhancing educational systems using evidence, assessment data, stakeholder feedback, and governance practices.

---

## Course

An organized sequence of learning activities designed to achieve specific learning outcomes within an academic program.

---

## Curriculum

The planned sequence of educational experiences, assessments, and learning activities that support learner development.

---

## Curriculum Engineering

The application of engineering principles, systems thinking, lifecycle management, governance, traceability, documentation, quality assurance, and continuous improvement to the design, development, delivery, assessment, and evolution of educational systems.

---

# D

## Deliver Phase

The phase of the Atlas Method in which engineered curriculum is delivered to learners.

---

## Design Phase

The phase of the Atlas Method during which educational architecture is transformed into detailed instructional designs.

---

## Develop Phase

The phase of the Atlas Method in which instructional materials, assessments, learning activities, and educational artifacts are created.

---

# E

## Educational Artifact

Any documented output produced during the curriculum engineering lifecycle.

Examples include:

- Program specifications
- Course specifications
- Lesson plans
- Assessments
- Rubrics
- Learning activities

---

## Educational System

A coordinated collection of educational components working together to achieve defined educational outcomes.

---

## Engineer Phase

The phase of the Atlas Method during which knowledge is transformed into structured educational architecture.

---

# K

## Knowledge Domain

A defined area of expertise that serves as the source material for curriculum development.

---

## Knowledge Transformation

The systematic process of converting subject matter expertise into structured educational experiences.

---

# L

## Learning Outcome

A measurable statement describing what learners should know or be able to do upon completion of an educational experience.

---

# M

## Module

A coherent unit of instruction within a course focused on a specific topic or set of learning outcomes.

---

# P

## Program Outcome

A measurable capability expected of graduates upon completion of an academic program.

---

# R

## Reference Implementation

A complete example demonstrating practical application of the Atlas Framework.

---

## Rubric

A structured scoring guide used to evaluate learner performance against defined criteria.

---

# S

## Specification

A detailed description explaining how Atlas Standards are implemented.

---

## Systems Thinking

An approach that views curriculum as an interconnected educational system rather than a collection of isolated courses or learning activities.

---

# T

## Template

A reusable document or artifact that supports consistent implementation of Atlas Standards and Specifications.

---

# Traceability

The ability to identify and document relationships between educational artifacts throughout the curriculum engineering lifecycle.

---

# Living Glossary

The Atlas Glossary is a living reference.

New terminology shall be incorporated as the discipline evolves while maintaining consistency across all Atlas publications.
