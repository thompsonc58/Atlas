## Introduction

The **Atlas Curriculum Engineering Framework (ACEF)** is a comprehensive
implementation framework for the emerging discipline of **Curriculum
Engineering**.

Curriculum Engineering is the discipline. **Atlas** is the framework
that organizes, applies, and advances that discipline through its
**Atlas Body of Knowledge (ABOK)**, methodologies, standards,
specifications, templates, governance model, reference implementations,
and software platform.

Rather than viewing curriculum as a collection of independent courses or
instructional materials, Curriculum Engineering treats curriculum as an
interconnected educational system. Atlas provides a structured
implementation of these principles, enabling educational systems to be
intentionally architected, governed, assessed, and continuously improved
throughout their lifecycle.

## The Atlas Vision

Atlas envisions a future in which Curriculum Engineering becomes a
recognized engineering discipline supported by a mature body of
knowledge, professional practice, research community, and implementation
ecosystem.

## The Atlas Framework

The Atlas Framework consists of six integrated components:

1.  **Atlas Handbook (ABOK)** --- the primary body of knowledge
    describing Curriculum Engineering.
2.  **Atlas Standards** --- normative requirements.
3.  **Atlas Specifications** --- implementation guidance.
4.  **Atlas Templates** --- reusable engineering artifacts.
5.  **Reference Implementations** --- practical demonstrations.
6.  **Atlas Studio** --- the software platform supporting
    implementation.

## The Atlas Method

The Atlas Method is the engineering lifecycle used to implement
Curriculum Engineering through the Discover, Engineer, Design, Develop,
Deliver, and Improve phases.
