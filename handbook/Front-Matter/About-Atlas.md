## Introduction

The **Atlas Curriculum Engineering Framework (ACEF)** is a comprehensive framework for designing, developing, governing, delivering, assessing, and continuously improving educational systems using the principles and practices of engineering.

Atlas introduces the emerging discipline of **Curriculum Engineering**, which applies systems thinking, lifecycle management, governance, quality assurance, documentation, traceability, and continuous improvement to curriculum development.

Rather than viewing curriculum as a collection of independent courses or instructional materials, Atlas treats curriculum as an interconnected educational system that can be intentionally architected, managed, evaluated, and evolved throughout its lifecycle.

---

# The Atlas Vision

Atlas envisions a future in which curriculum development is approached with the same rigor, discipline, and continuous improvement practices that have transformed mature engineering professions.

By combining educational theory, instructional design, systems engineering, and software engineering practices, Atlas seeks to establish a repeatable methodology for engineering high-quality educational systems.

---

# The Atlas Framework

The Atlas Curriculum Engineering Framework consists of six integrated components.

## 1. Atlas Handbook

The **Atlas Handbook** serves as the authoritative body of knowledge for Curriculum Engineering.

It defines the philosophy, terminology, methodology, engineering models, and foundational concepts that support the framework.

The handbook should be read before applying Atlas standards or using Atlas Studio.

---

## 2. Atlas Standards

Atlas Standards define the normative requirements for implementing the framework.

These standards establish consistent expectations for educational artifacts, documentation, governance, quality assurance, and lifecycle management.

---

## 3. Atlas Specifications

Specifications describe how standards are implemented in practice.

They provide detailed guidance for engineering academic programs, courses, modules, lessons, assessments, rubrics, and related educational artifacts.

---

## 4. Atlas Templates

Templates provide reusable starting points for implementing Atlas standards and specifications.

They promote consistency while reducing the effort required to develop high-quality curriculum artifacts.

---

## 5. Reference Implementations

Reference Implementations demonstrate the Atlas Framework in practice.

They provide complete examples of academic programs engineered using Atlas principles and serve as models for future implementations.

The Software Development Associate of Applied Science program is the initial reference implementation for the framework.

---

## 6. Atlas Studio

Atlas Studio is the software platform that automates the Atlas Method.

It assists educators and curriculum developers by managing educational artifacts, enforcing engineering standards, maintaining traceability, supporting governance, and enabling continuous improvement throughout the curriculum lifecycle.

---

# The Atlas Method

At the core of the framework is the **Atlas Method**, a structured engineering lifecycle for transforming domain expertise into measurable learning experiences.

The Atlas Method consists of six phases:

- Discover
- Engineer
- Design
- Develop
- Deliver
- Improve

Each phase produces defined artifacts and contributes to the continuous evolution of educational systems.
